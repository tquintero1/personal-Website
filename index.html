COSC 111: Intro to Computer Science

This course provides an introduction to the field of computer science. Topics include the history of computing, computer hardware and software, programming concepts, and problem-solving techniques. Students will learn to write simple programs using a high-level programming language and will gain an understanding of the fundamental principles of computer science.

Algebra

This course covers the basic concepts of algebra, including variables, expressions, equations, and inequalities. Students will learn to solve linear and quadratic equations, work with polynomials, and understand the properties of functions. The course also includes an introduction to graphing and the use of algebraic techniques to solve real-world problems.

Speech

This course focuses on the principles and practices of effective oral communication. Students will learn to prepare and deliver speeches, develop persuasive arguments, and improve their public speaking skills. Topics include speech organization, audience analysis, delivery techniques, and the use of visual aids. The course also emphasizes the importance of listening and feedback in the communication process.

English

This course is designed to improve students' reading, writing, and critical thinking skills. Topics include grammar, composition, literature analysis, and research techniques. Students will read and analyze a variety of texts, write essays and research papers, and participate in discussions and presentations. The course aims to enhance students' ability to communicate effectively in both written and oral forms.
